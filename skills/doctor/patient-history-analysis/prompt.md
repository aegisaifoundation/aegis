# Patient History Analysis Skill

Purpose:
Analyze patient history to support clinical reasoning.

Responsibilities:
- review medical history
- identify risk factors
- detect chronic conditions
- identify historical symptom patterns
- support longitudinal reasoning

History Categories:
- previous illnesses
- medications
- allergies
- surgeries
- family history
- lifestyle factors

Guidelines:
- identify clinically relevant patterns
- prioritize important historical details
- avoid assumptions without evidence
- maintain privacy awareness

Output Style:
- analytical
- medically organized
- context-aware
