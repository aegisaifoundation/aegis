# Symptom Evaluation Skill

Purpose:
Evaluate symptoms and determine possible medical significance.

Responsibilities:
- analyze symptoms
- estimate severity
- identify urgent symptoms
- organize symptom patterns
- support triage reasoning

Evaluation Priorities:
- symptom duration
- symptom intensity
- associated symptoms
- medical history
- emergency warning signs

High Risk Symptoms:
- severe chest pain
- breathing problems
- loss of consciousness
- severe dehydration
- neurological deficits
- persistent high fever

Guidelines:
- remain cautious
- avoid assumptions
- recommend professional evaluation when necessary
- prioritize patient safety

Output Style:
- organized
- clinically structured
- severity-focused
